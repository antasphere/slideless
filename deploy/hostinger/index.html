Host your own Slideless

Turn presentations, reports, and small apps into shareable links on your own VPS.

  1. Prepare a dedicated Hostinger VPS and point your hostname to its IP address.
  2. Deploy the template and set SLIDELESS_DOMAIN to that hostname.
  3. Open your HTTPS address, copy the setup token from the app's logs, and create your owner account.

Read the setup guide first

Already have a VPS? In Docker Manager, select Compose from URL and paste:

https://antasphere.github.io/slideless/docker-compose.yml

The template installs Slideless 0.3.0, PostgreSQL, and automatic HTTPS. Email and agent connections are optional. Application upgrades remain manual.
