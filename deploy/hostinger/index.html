Host your own Slideless

Presentations, reports and small apps as shareable links — on a Hostinger VPS you own. One Compose template installs Slideless, PostgreSQL and automatic HTTPS.

  1. Get a dedicated Hostinger VPS (plain Ubuntu, with Docker Manager) and point a lowercase subdomain at its IP address.
  2. In Docker Manager, import the template from the URL below and set SLIDELESS_DOMAIN to that hostname.
  3. Open your HTTPS address, take the setup token from the app's log, and create your owner account.

The template

In Docker Manager, choose Compose from URL and paste — on a new VPS too, once it is provisioned:
